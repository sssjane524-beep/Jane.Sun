Overview

A global distributor conference of approximately 500 people required communications, physical materials and guest arrangements to come together on site. I participated throughout planning and delivery, with a focus on communications, materials and related operational coordination.

My contribution

Content and design

Structured event communications and material use cases, working with designers on messaging hierarchy, revisions and delivery needs.

Physical materials and vendors

Coordinated with vendors to produce and deliver staff apparel, customized gift packs and gifts.

Hotel and overseas guests

Coordinated hotel arrangements and overseas guest reception, connecting requests, venue flow and the responsible teams.

On-site distribution

Worked with the on-site team to organize arrival, sorting and distribution so materials were available for the relevant moments.
